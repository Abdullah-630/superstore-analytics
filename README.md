# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analytics

An end-to-end exploratory and diagnostic data analysis project uncovering actionable business insights on sales performance, customer segments, profit margins, and supply chain shipping efficiency.

---

## Project Overview
The Superstore dataset contains multi-year transactional data for a retail chain across various regions and product categories. The primary goal of this project is to evaluate business performance, identify high/low-margin drivers, and deliver data-backed recommendations to optimize revenue and operational profitability.

---

## Business Problems & Key Objectives
1. **Sales & Profit Optimization:** Identify which product categories and sub-categories generate the highest revenue vs. profit margins.
2. **Discount Sensitivity:** Analyze the impact of aggressive discounting on bottom-line profitability.
3. **Customer Segmentation:** Understand purchasing behaviors across `Consumer`, `Corporate`, and `Home Office` segments.
4. **Geographic & Shipping Performance:** Pinpoint underperforming regions/states and analyze delivery delays by `Ship Mode`.
